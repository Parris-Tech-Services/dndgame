## Assistant

Here’s a **council plan that “caters to everyone”** *as much as is actually possible* in Baldur’s Gate… while still letting tension simmer and giving you clean hooks for betrayal, alliances, and power plays.

## The Mirtul Accord (your wizard-crafted future for Baldur’s Gate)

### 1) Non-negotiables (you set the tone)

- **No civil war in the streets.** Any faction that starts one becomes *the* enemy.
- **No infernal pacts inside city governance.** Existing pacts get disclosed to a sealed registry (kept by you + a neutral witness).
- **No shadow-armies.** Religious practice is tolerated; organised recruitment for “grief militias” is not.
- **All sign under Arcane Oath.** *Geas / Mark of Binding / Zone of Truth* during signing. Everyone knows you can enforce it.

This instantly pressures **Ulder** (order), **Helm** (duty), **Harpers** (rights), and puts **Zhent/Sharrans/Vanthampur** on a leash without humiliating them.

## 2) Three Pillars everyone can argue under (but still sign)

### Pillar A — Security Charter (Ulder + Helm + Harper)

**What it does**
- Restores **Flaming Fist command to Ulder** with a public mandate: gates, patrols, anti-cult operations.
- **Helm cleric gets Watchtower Authority**: audits, inspections, readiness drills, ward maintenance.
- **Harper gets a Civil Oversight Seat** + protected informant channel to stop tyranny.

**Your wizard add-ons**
- Wards on major gates against devils/undead/shadow incursions.
- A “blue lantern” alarm system (arcane beacons) across districts.
- *Truth-binding* for senior officers to reduce corruption.

**Who likes it**
- Ulder: “strength + legitimacy”
- Helm: “vigilance”
- Harpers: “rights + checks”
- Lady Shaela: “safer lower districts”

### Pillar B — Trade Concord (Guild + Zhent + Vanthampur + commons)

**What it does**
- **Tariff reform**: lower baseline tariffs for honest trade; heavier penalties for smuggling/price-gouging.
- **Guild runs the markets**, but under anti-monopoly rules.
- **Zhents get legal cover** *only* as “licensed security and courier contractors” for caravans—profit, but leashed.
- **Vanthampur gets the Commerce Chair** *if* she funds a public works bond and submits to audits.

**Your wizard add-ons**
- A **Teleport Circle Permit System**: limited circles for regulated trade (you control the keys).
- Arcane stamps on contracts to prevent forged ledgers.
- “Fair coin” wards to detect counterfeit.

**Who likes it**
- Guild: fair markets, reduced tariffs, anti-monopoly
- Zhent: profit + influence with legitimacy (and a knife-edge to fall from)
- Thalamra: control + “stability” + political chair
- Lady Shaela: relief + lower district investment

### Pillar C — Mercy & Shadows (Raphina + Viconia + Shar problem)

This is the powder keg. Your plan should *offer a bridge* that most can accept—while making it very clear **Shar’s “army of grief”** is a line you won’t cross.

**What it does**
- **Infernal Compact Taskforce** (Raphina’s win): investigate pacts, break chains, protect innocents from devils.
- **A sanctioned House of Night’s Rest** (Viconia’s win): a grief sanctuary *licensed* as hospice/rites of mourning—no recruitment, no armed wing, monitored.
- **A citywide “Rites of Remembrance” program**: gives people a healthy outlet for grief so Shar can’t harvest despair as easily.

**Your wizard add-ons**
- A sealed infernal registry (names hidden unless a pact threatens the city).
- Anti-scry wards for victims so devils can’t easily reclaim them.
- A “Shadow Containment Clause”: anyone caught forming a shadow-army gets exiled or bound.

**Who likes it**
- Raphina: direct path to freedom/redemption
- Viconia: legitimate influence through grief rites (but constrained)
- Harpers/Helm/Ulder: cult containment
- The commons: real support

**Shar**
- Shar will *pretend* to accept, then test your enforcement.
- Give Shar something small but controlled: **permission to maintain a single “Shrine of Quiet”** *without recruitment*.
- Privately: you prepare a contingency to expose/banish her influence when she violates terms.

## 3) “What everyone gets / what everyone gives” (quick bargaining map)

- **Thalamra** gets Commerce Chair + stability. Gives: audits, public works funding, no covert coups.
- **Raphina** gets infernal taskforce + protection + pact-breaking priority. Gives: transparency about infernal threats, cooperation with Fist.
- **Viconia** gets licensed grief sanctuary + social influence. Gives: no recruitment, no violence, accepts oversight.
- **Ulder** gets restored command + resources + mandate. Gives: accepts oversight, no martial law without council vote.
- **Harpers** get oversight seat + protections. Gives: won’t sabotage lawful trade/security reforms.
- **Zhents** get legal contracts (profit). Gives: no Guild war, no assassinations inside city, submit to inspections.
- **Guild** gets tariff reform + anti-monopoly enforcement. Gives: fair pricing, labour protections, transparent ledgers.
- **Helm** gets watchtower authority + gate vigilance. Gives: no inquisitorial overreach, obeys civil law.
- **Lady Shaela** gets relief funding + representation reforms. Gives: mobilises peers behind the accord.
